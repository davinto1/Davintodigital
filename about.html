About Us

From a Students’ Community to a Digital Brand

DavintoDigital is a Nigeria-based digital technology and media brand helping individuals, small businesses, personal brands, student entrepreneurs and organisations build a clearer, more useful and more credible presence online.

Its services bring together web development, search visibility, digital marketing, media and content, WhatsApp business workflows, automation and practical custom digital solutions.

The brand focuses on the practical side of being online: presenting a business clearly, making information easier to find, creating useful content, improving customer communication and building digital tools around real needs.

Meet the Founder

Chima Chimdindu MacDonald

Popularly known as Davinto

A Nigerian student and digital entrepreneur whose interests span technology, online communication, web development, search visibility, digital media and practical tools for solving everyday problems.

He began building the brand from a student-community environment, where affordability, clear communication and usefulness matter. His approach is to understand what a person or organisation is trying to achieve, then identify a digital solution that fits the goal and budget.

The name DavintoDigital combines the founder’s familiar identity, Davinto, with the brand’s focus on digital work.
